# Content reference

SlimefunWarfare expands the Slimefun modality with a late-game military technology tree.
All recipes and item identifiers remain compatible with the original addon.

## Progression

1. Extract arsenic, monazite and rare-earth resources.
2. Process materials in the Air Liquefier, Bullet Press, Explosive Synthesizer and Ion Exchange Separator.
3. Craft conventional and energy weapons with specialized ammunition.
4. Build modular powered armor and install modules through the Element Forge.
5. Use meteor technology to obtain the highest-tier materials.

## Systems

- Firearms, energy rifles and ammunition with range, minimum-range and reload rules.
- Chemical grenades with distinct visual and status effects.
- Energy blades and reinforced concrete.
- Modular power armor with flight, life support, protection, movement and utility modules.
- Rare-earth processing, meteor attraction and an elemental reactor.
- Radioactive materials handled through Slimefun's native radiation API.

## DrakesCraft safety policy

- Content is enabled only in `world`, `world_nether` and `world_the_end` by default.
- Classic, SkyBlock, OneBlock, hubs and boss arenas reject Warfare activation.
- Protection checks are mandatory for attacks and world interactions.
- Warfare explosions do not break blocks.
- Nuclear activation is disabled by default and requires an explicit configuration change.
- Automatic third-party updates and telemetry are disabled.

See [`config.yml`](../src/main/resources/config.yml) for all runtime controls.
